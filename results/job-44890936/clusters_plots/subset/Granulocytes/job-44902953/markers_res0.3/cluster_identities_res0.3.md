## Interpretation and limits

The proposed labels summarize this resolution's own marker evidence. They are not saved cell annotations or experimental lineage validation. Keep/flag/remove are recommendations only, and no cells were removed or reassigned.
The 25 panels in `R/clusters/markers.R` provided candidate vocabulary. Historical panel cluster numbers and human EVLP signatures did not determine these labels. Only the 39 feature aliases frozen in this marker producer's `.INFO` were used. No inferred orthologues or cross-resolution label transfer were used.
Positive identity genes have adjusted p < 0.05 and positive avg_log2FC here. Canonical genes also include lineage comparisons and diagnostics; their evidence status is explicit. `pct.1` and `pct.2` are detection fractions in the cluster and its pooled rest, not proportions of a lineage. Missing rows cannot establish absent expression, and negative fold change is depletion rather than absence.
No marker-only assessment certifies the absence of individual doublets, ambient RNA, batch effects or donor effects. The data do not establish treatment responses or developmental transitions. No new DE, filtering, normalization, clustering or plotting was performed.
