The proposed labels summarize this resolution's own marker evidence. They are not saved cell annotations or experimental lineage validation. Keep/flag/remove are recommendations only, and no cells were removed or reassigned.
The 25 panels in `R/clusters/markers.R` provided candidate vocabulary. Historical panel cluster numbers and human EVLP signatures did not determine these labels. Only the 39 feature aliases frozen in this marker producer's `.INFO` were used. No inferred orthologues or cross-resolution label transfer were used.
