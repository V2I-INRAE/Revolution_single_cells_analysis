## Concise Cluster Rationale

* **Multiciliated (`C1` = `2,116`, `C5` = `842`)**: Both are `FOXJ1⁺` ciliated cells; C1 is enriched for apical ciliary-tip/radial-spoke transcripts (`SNTN`, `TPPP3`, `CAPS`, `RSPH4A`), whereas C5 is enriched for long axonemal dynein heavy chains and RFX TFs (`DNAH2/3/5/7`, `PACRG`, `CFAP54`, `RFX2/3`).
* **Club / Secretory (`C2` = `1,859`, `C3` = `1,342`)**: C2 captures distal/respiratory-bronchiole secretory cells co-expressing Club (`SCGB3A2`, `SCGB1A1`, `PIGR`) and alveolar surfactant/pepsinogen genes (`SFTPB`, `SFTPA1`, `PGC`, `ATP13A5`, `ALPL`; Basil et al. 2022 `PMID: 35355013`), whereas C3 captures proximal `SCGB1A1^{hi} SCGB3A1^{hi} S100A2⁺` Club/Secretory cells.
* **Alveolar Pneumocytes (`C4` AT2 = `1,287`, `C6` AT1 = `662`)**: C4 expresses canonical surfactant machinery (`SFTPC` `99.5%`, `SFTPA1/B/D`, `LAMP3`, `ABCA3`, `NAPSA`, `SLC34A2`); C6 expresses gas-exchange AT1 markers (`AGER` `98%`, `RTKN2`, `CAV1/2`, `COL4A3/4`, `HOPX`, `AQP5`, `CLIC5`).
* **Tuft-2 (`C8` = `448`) vs. Tuft-1 (`C9` = `320`)**: Both share `POU2F3` (`95%`), `POU2AF2`, `AVIL`, `SH2D7`, and `ALOX5`; **C8 (Tuft-2)** is specialized for succinate/alarmin sensing and leukotriene synthesis (`SUCNR1`, `ALOX5AP^{hi}`, `IL17RB^{hi}`, `GFI1B`, `SPIB`, `PTPRC/CD45⁺`; Montoro et al. 2018 `PMID: 30069044`; Perniss et al. 2023 `PMID: 37531421`), whereas **C9 (Tuft-1)** is cholinergic/neuronal-like (`CHAT^{hi}`, `RGS21^{hi}`, `DCLK2^{hi}`, `NAV2^{hi}`, `HTR4⁺`).
* **Ionocytes (`C10` = `292`) & Basal (`C11` = `258`)**: C10 is defined by `FOXI1` (`91.8%`), `CFTR` (`79.8%`), and V-ATPase subunits (`ATP6V1G3`, `ATP6V1C2`); C11 is defined by `TP63`, `KRT5`, `KRT17`, `COL17A1`, and `ITGA2/B4`.
* **Doublets (`C7` = `603`, `REMOVE`)**: T/NK leukocyte (`PTPRC`, `CD2`, `CD3D`, `GZMA`, `CCL5`) + Epithelial doublets.
