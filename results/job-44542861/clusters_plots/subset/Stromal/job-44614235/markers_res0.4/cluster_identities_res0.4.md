## Concise Cluster Rationale

* **Interstitial / Adventitial Fibroblast Axis (`C4` = `1,438`, `C1` = `4,556`)**: **C4** captures steady-state universal adventitial/subpleural fibroblasts (`PI16^{hi} MFAP5^{hi} PCOLCE2^{hi} CLEC3B^{hi} DPP4⁺ PROCR⁺ ACKR2/3/4⁺ IL33⁺`; Buechler et al. 2021 `PMID: 33981032`), whereas **C1** captures EVLP-activated `COL14A1^{hi} CFD^{hi} C7^{hi} LUM^{hi}` matrix fibroblasts upregulating `IGF1`, `IL6`, `PTGS2 (COX-2)`, `PTGES`, `SERPINE1`, `ADAMTS4`, and `CXCL12`.
* **Alveolar & Epithelial-Niche Fibroblast Axis (`C3` = `1,617`, `C5` = `529`)**: **C3** represents canonical alveolar septal lipofibroblasts (`COL13A1^{hi} ITGA8^{hi} NPNT^{hi} TCF21^{hi} APOE⁺ LIMCH1^{hi} A2M^{hi} BMPER⁺ TF⁺`; Travaglini et al. 2020 `PMID: 33208946`), while **C5** represents specialized `LGR5^{hi} SFRP1^{hi} FGF10^{hi} HGF^{hi} FST^{hi} CRABP1^{hi} HEYL⁺ FGFR2⁺` epithelial-niche mesenchymal cells (Lee et al. 2017 `PMID: 28886383`).
* **Contractile & Vascular Mural Axes (`C2` = `2,751`, `C7` = `188`)**: **C2** (`PDGFRA⁺ HHIP⁺`) combines `WIF1^{hi} GREM1^{hi} CLDN11^{hi} BDNF⁺ NDNF^{hi}` alveolar ductal myofibroblasts and `MYH11^{hi} ACTA2^{hi}` airway smooth muscle (Narvaez Del Pilar et al. 2022 `PMID: 35302583`), whereas **C7** (`PDGFRB^{hi} PDGFRA⁻`) captures vascular mural pericytes (`NOTCH3^{hi} MCAM^{hi} ABCC9⁺ KCNJ8⁺ COX4I2⁺ CSPG4⁺`) and VSMCs (`ACTA2⁺ MYH11⁺ TINAGL1^{hi} GUCY1B1^{hi}`).
* **Doublets (`C6` = `319`, `REMOVE`)**: Leukocyte (`PTPRC`, `SRGN`, `LAPTM5`, `CCL5`, `CD2`, `CD3D`) + Fibroblast (`COL1A1`, `COL3A1`, `DCN`) doublets.
