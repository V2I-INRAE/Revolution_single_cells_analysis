## Concise Cluster Rationale

* **`CD2⁻ WC1⁺ (CD163L1⁺) SOX13^{hi} GATA3^{hi} BLK⁺ RHEX^{hi}` Porcine $\gamma\delta$ T-Cell Lineage (`Clusters 1–6` = `19,998` cells, `95.90%`)**:
  * **Control-to-EVLP Activation Trajectory (`C1` $\rightarrow$ `C2` & `C6`)**: **C1 (`7,092`)** is the resting/recirculating `SELL^{hi} S1PR1⁺ KLF2^{hi} ID3⁺ ITGB7⁺ CD27⁺ GATA3^{hi}` pool enriched in unperfused Control lungs (`66.6%` of all Control cells; Wiarda et al. 2022 `PMID: 35995567`). During EVLP, these cells transition into **C2 (`5,555`, `98.5%` EVLP)**, upregulating `CD69`, `CXCR4`, `CCR7`, `SGK1`, `BCL2`, and `IL18R1`, and **C6 (`587`, `95.9%` EVLP)**, which blasts and upregulates `TNFRSF9 (4-1BB)`, `TNFRSF18 (GITR)`, `TNFSF11 (RANKL)`, `IL2RA (CD25)`, `IRF4`, and porcine $\gamma\delta$ T-cell APC co-stimulatory molecules `CD80` and `CD83` (Takamatsu et al. 2006 `PMID: 17389056`).
  * **Tissue-Resident Effector-Memory & Quiescent States (`C3`, `C4`, `C5`)**: **C3 (`2,935`)** is a `CD27⁻ SELL⁻` mucosal effector-memory state (`CXCR6⁺ TIGIT⁺ JAML⁺ CRTAM⁺ CD96⁺ ICOS⁺ PRDM1⁺ BHLHE40⁺ ID2^{hi} RORA⁺ TNF⁺ CCL5⁺ FCGR3A⁺ MHC-II⁺`); **C4 (`2,096`)** is a chromatin-poised `SOX13^{hi} BLK^{hi} FYN^{hi} BACH2^{hi} TCF12^{hi} BCL11B⁺ ZBTB16⁺` state; **C5 (`1,733`)** is a low-UMI basal state.
* **`CD2⁺ WC1⁻ EOMES^{hi} IKZF2^{hi}` Porcine $\gamma\delta$ T-Cell Lineage (`Cluster 7` = `455` cells, `2.18%`)**: Matches the developmentally distinct `CD2⁺ WC1⁻ EOMES⁺ CD27⁺ IKZF2(Helios)⁺` porcine lung $\gamma\delta$ T-cell subset (Rodríguez-Gómez et al. 2019 `PMID: 30915070`; Zhang et al. 2024 `PMID: 38384475`), co-expressing `CD3D/E/G⁺`, `LCK^{hi}`, `FCRL6^{hi}`, `ADGRG1⁺`, `TOX^{hi}`, `IL2RB^{hi}`, `CRTAM⁺`, `KIT⁺`, and `NPY^{hi}`.
* **Pulmonary Group 2 Innate Lymphoid Cells (`ILC2`: `Cluster 8` = `399` cells, `1.91%`)**: Strictly `CD3D/E/G⁻ BLK⁻ CD163L1⁻` innate lymphoid cells (`CD2⁺ CD3E⁻ ID2^{hi} KIT^{hi} IL7R⁺`; Wiarda et al. 2022 `PMID: 35995567`) expressing the canonical `ILC2` program (`GATA3^{hi} RORA⁺ ZBTB16/PLZF⁺ MAF^{hi} HLF^{hi} HOPX^{hi} IL2RA/CD25^{hi} IL1RL1/ST2⁺ PTGDR2/CRTH2⁺ ICOS⁺`).
