## Concise Cluster Rationale

* **Porcine Alveolar Macrophages (`PAMs`: `C1` = `16,244`, `C2` = `5,933`, `C3` = `5,559`)**: Porcine PAM markers distribute unevenly across the three states: `ENPP1/CD203a` is a significant row in all three (C1 #66, C2 #1172, C3 #57); `LGALS3` and `FCN2` likewise (C1 #668/#93, C2 #36/#409, C3 #38/#146); `CYP7A1` is strongly enriched in C1 (#2) and enriched in C3 (#43) but depleted in C2 (#3001, `log2FC = −1.74`); `MRC1` is enriched in C1 (#435) and depleted in C2 (#2484) with no significant row in C3; `PPARG` is a significant row only in C1 (#642) and C2 (#1687). So PAM assignment of C2/C3 rests on the shared `ENPP1/LGALS3/FCN2` program, not on `MRC1/PPARG` universality. **C1** matches steady-state phagocytic PAMs (`NREP` #1, `CYP7A1` #2, `NAAA` #11, `FCAR/CD89` #14, `LYZ`, `BHLHE41` #30, `FCGR1A/CD64` #32, `SIGLEC1/CD169` #109). **C2** is the late-EVLP lipid-remodeling state (`CD24` #3, `OCSTAMP` #4, `FABP5` #5, `EPCAM` #6, `LPL` #11; `5,880 / 5,933` cells from non-control samples, `99.1%`); `EPCAM` here is carried over from the first-pass fusion-associated macrophage interpretation, with epithelial contamination as the explicitly unresolved alternative (see QC caveat). **C3** is a quiescent/basal PAM state with the flattest profile (top `log2FC = 1.04`; `MRC1`/`PPARG` not significant rows). *(Note: Porcine `CD172a^{hi} CD163^{hi}` `AM-like` PIMs do not separate from AMs in bulk RNA-seq [Crisci et al. 2020 `PMID: 33365028`] and may co-cluster here).*
* **Interstitial / Perivascular Macrophages (`C4` = `5,420`)**: Conserved `DNASE1L3` (#1, `log2FC = 8.21`) / `CCL2` (#8) / `CCL8` (#21) / `CCL14` (#31) `MAF^{hi}` (#16, `78.9%`) program with `IGF1` (`log2FC = 7.74`), `STAB1`/`MSR1` scavenger receptors, `VCAM1` (#38), `MRC1` (#374, `95.0%`), `C1QA/B/C` (#124–300, `92–94%`), and `HMOX1` (#287).
* **Classical Monocyte & `MoMac` Maturation Continuum (`C6` = `1,827`, `C5` = `4,416`, `C8` = `988`)**: Spans from `RETN^{hi}` (#1) `CD14^{hi} SELL⁺ CSF3R^{hi} GPR84⁺ TREM1⁺ IL1B^{hi}` classical monocytes (`cMo`, **C6**) to `PLBD1^{hi} AREG^{hi} EREG^{hi}` `CCR2⁺ IL1R1/2⁺` tissue-repair monocytes/early `MoMacs` (**C5**) and `LRMDA⁺ SH3PXD2B⁺ TBXAS1^{hi}` differentiating `MoMacs` (**C8**). **C5 EC-signal note**: `PECAM1` (#18, `41.9%`) with mast-associated `MS4A2` (#19, `52.6%`) / `MILR1` (#20, `35.3%`) in the top 25 — this signal was present in the prior run's CSVs (`PECAM1` #35, `MS4A2` #18, `MILR1` #19) and is now explicitly flagged as an annotated caveat, not a new doublet call; `EPCAM` is depleted (#3499) and `CSF3R` has no significant row. **C8**: `MERTK` has no significant row and is omitted from the current identity label pending direct expression assessment.
* **`MARCO^{hi} TIMD4⁺` Macrophages (`C7` = `1,137`)**: `IGFBP2` (#1, `log2FC = 11.32`) / `MARCO` (#2, `log2FC = 9.86`) with `TIMD4` (#11, `45.3%`), `IDO1` (#8), `CCL19` (#5), `SLAMF7` (#33), `PDCD1LG2` (#37), `C1QA/B` (#139/#163), and `VSIG4` (#305); `ENPP1`/`MRC1`-depleted and capillary EC co-detection (`PLVAP` #9 `79.1%`, `CDH5` #4 `37.9%`; `KIT`/`PECAM1` no significant rows). Kept with provisional anatomical attribution, as in the first pass. The removed first-pass C9 (`ncMo` + EC complex) and C10 (multi-lineage soup) signatures do not reappear as clusters.
