## Concise Cluster Rationale

* **Merged PAM States (C1 = `22,898`)**: Homeostatic (`CYP7A1` #24, `NAAA` #141, `FCAR/CD89` #13, `SIGLEC1/CD169` #221, `LYZ`, `BHLHE41`) and quiescent/basal (`FCN2` #5, `ACKR4`, `BTC`) PAM signals co-cluster with the pan-PAM program (`ENPP1/CD203a` #28, `LGALS3` #32, `FABP5` #41, `MRC1` #1381 `96.4%`, `PPARG` #598 `93.9%`) plus the `EPCAM` signal (#9) discussed in the caveat. Because old quiescent C3 shares its `MYO1D`/`SLC16A10` program with new C4, the exact partition of the basal program between new C1 and C4 is barcode-unverified.
* **Inflammatory Monocytes (C2 = `6,232`)**: `IL1R2` (#1) / `RETN` (#2) led program with `IL1B` (#14), `AREG`/`EREG` (#10/#9), `GPR84` (#18), `SELL` (#23), `VCAN` (#26), `F13A1` (#41), `CD14` (#105), `FCN1` (#275); `CCR2`/`TREM1` have no significant rows at this resolution. Count-compatible with merger of old repair-monocyte and classical-monocyte clusters.
* **Interstitial Macrophages (C3 = `5,420`)**: Identical count to res-`0.4` C4 (`DNASE1L3`/`IGF1`/`CCL2`/`CCL8`/`CCL14`/`MAF`/`VCAM1`/`STAB1` program retained).
* **Weak Tail (C4 = `4,851`)** and **MoMac/`MARCO^{hi}` (C6 = `986`, C5 = `1,137`)**: as tabled; C5 count-identical to res-`0.4` C7 and C6 count-compatible with res-`0.4` C8.
