## Concise Cluster Rationale

* **Porcine Alveolar Macrophages (`PAMs`: `C1` = `15,848`, `C2` = `6,309`, `C3` = `5,578`)**: Exclusively express canonical porcine PAM markers `ENPP1/CD203a` (`74–98%`), `CLEC12B` (`24–59%`), `PLSCR1` (`61–97%`), `PPARG` (`62–94%`), `BHLHE41` (`79%` in C1), `MRC1` (`96–97%`), `SIGLEC1` (`86%` in C1), and `FCN2` (`94–99%`) (Álvarez et al. 2023 `PMID: 37626913`; Poderoso et al. 2020 `PMID: 32535044`; Chen et al. 2025 `PMID: 40242774`). **C1** matches steady-state `MRC1-NREP^{high}` (`PON1⁺ PPBP⁺`) BALF PAMs (Shin et al. 2025 `PMID: 39875369`); **C2** is a Late-EVLP (`8–10h`) lipid-remodeling/fusion-active PAM state (`FABP5^{hi} LPL^{hi} GPD1⁺ OCSTAMP⁺ CD24⁺ EPCAM⁺`; `6,248 / 6,309` cells from EVLP lungs); **C3** is a quiescent PAM state. *(Note: Porcine `CD172a^{hi} CD163^{hi}` `AM-like` PIMs do not separate from AMs in bulk RNA-seq [Crisci et al. 2020 `PMID: 33365028`] and may co-cluster here).*
* **Interstitial / Perivascular Macrophages (`C4` = `5,434`)**: Conserved `C1QA/B/C^{hi} CCL8⁺ CCL14⁺ CCL2⁺ MAF^{hi} MAFB^{hi} FCGR2B^{hi} STAB1⁺ MSR1⁺ MRC1^{hi} CD163^{hi} VCAM1⁺ HMOX1^{hi} ACP5⁺ DNASE1L3⁺ IGF1⁺` interstitial macrophages (Mulder et al. 2021 `PMID: 34297922`).
* **Classical Monocyte & `MoMac` Maturation Continuum (`C6` = `1,727`, `C5` = `4,466`, `C8` = `1,024`)**: Spans from `CD14^{hi} CD163^{lo} SELL⁺ CSF3R^{hi} RETN^{hi} GPR84⁺ TREM1⁺ IL1B^{hi}` classical monocytes (`cMo`, **C6**) to `CD14⁺ CD163^{hi} F13A1^{hi} VCAN^{hi} FCN1^{hi} PLBD1^{hi} AREG^{hi} EREG^{hi} IL1B^{hi} IL1R1/2⁺ CCR2⁺` tissue-repair monocytes/early `MoMacs` (**C5**) and `F13A1^{hi} FCGR3A^{hi} CSF1R⁺ MERTK⁺ MITF⁺ LRMDA⁺ SH3PXD2B⁺ TBXAS1^{hi} GAB2^{hi} JAK2^{hi}` differentiating `MoMacs` (**C8**) (Fairbairn et al. 2013 `PMID: 23667115`; Gouin et al. 2023 `PMID: 37465668`).
* **`MARCO^{hi} TIMD4⁺ VSIG4⁺ C4BPA^{hi} C1Q^{hi}` Macrophages (`C7` = `1,138`) vs. Patrolling `ncMo`–EC Complex (`C9` = `445`, `REMOVE`) & Multi-Lineage Doublets (`C10` = `245`, `REMOVE`)**: **C7** combines `MARCO^{hi} SLAMF7^{hi} PDCD1LG2^{hi}` (reported in porcine BAL PAM subsets; Ke et al. 2023 `PMID: 37078873`; Zong et al. 2025 `PMID: 40944943`; Shin et al. 2025 `PMID: 39875369`), `MARCO⁺ CD163⁺ C1QA/B/C⁺` (annotated as `IM` in piglet lung scRNA-seq; Zhang et al. 2026 `PMID: 41843736`), and `TIMD4⁺ VSIG4⁺ C4BPA^{hi} IDO1⁺ CCL19⁺` with capillary EC co-detection (`PLVAP` `79%`, `CDH5` `38%`); kept with provisional anatomical attribution. **C9** lacks `MARCO/TIMD4/VSIG4/MRC1` and is a patrolling `FCGR3A^{hi} CD14^{lo} POU2F2⁺ KLF2⁺` `ncMo` + `gCap` EC doublet/complex (`REMOVE`). **C10** is a multi-lineage doublet cluster (`REMOVE`).
